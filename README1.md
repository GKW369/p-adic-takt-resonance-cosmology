# pTRC Framework: Discrete Integer Computational Physics Engine

**Architecture Designed by Kurt Guido Wirminghaus**  
An infinitely scalable, deterministic integer graphics and simulation engine operating within the strict algebraic ring of integers (\(\mathbb{Z}\)). Bypasses floating-point accumulation noise, rounding-error divergences, and FPU-latency. Zero float pollution.

---

## 🔬 Core Mathematical Architecture
*   **The Modulo-24 Universal Clock Architecture:** Multi-axis spatial orientation and synaptic signal activations operate within the reduced residue class group of the cyclic Modulo-24 Architecture (\(\mathbb{Z}/24\mathbb{Z}\)).
*   **Multi-Tiered Primorial Sieve Scaling:** Spatial resolution and grid density are scaled without fractional scaling factors via concentric primorial rings (Modulo 210, Modulo 2310).
*   **Holographic Inversion:** Spatial depth and coordinate matrices alternate dynamically via orthogonal crossed planes, ensuring 100% information conservation.
